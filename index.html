Three things worth knowing before you scroll

A repository is a public folder of software. Stars are bookmarks from developers. Trending ranks what got bookmarked most in a day, a week or a month — it measures attention, not quality, and definitely not safety.

Much of this is not software you install. A growing share is a text file that tells an AI assistant how to do a job. You hand the file to Claude or ChatGPT, and the behaviour changes. No coding involved.

Almost all of it is AI. AI tooling, AI skills, AI apps — that is simply what GitHub’s front page looks like now. The useful question is which AI, and for whom.
